Website

Add this to the head of every page. It sets no cookies and is under 2 KB.

Page views count on their own, and so do clicks on .dmg, .zip and other files (download) and on Polar and Stripe checkout links (checkout). For anything else, add data-track="name" to a link or call statiku("name").

Domains

Pages on these hostnames and their subdomains can send events to this project. Anything else is refused.

Change them with npm run project -- domains example.com

Privacy

No cookies and nothing stored in the browser. IP addresses are used in memory for the country, then dropped. A visitor is a hash that changes every day and can't be traced back.

To leave your own visits out, run localStorage.statiku_ignore = "1" in your browser's console on your site.
