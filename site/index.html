Questions

Good to know.

Do I need a cookie banner for Statiku?

Statiku sets no cookies, stores nothing on your visitors' devices and keeps no IP addresses, which is why sites using it usually don't show a banner for it. Your own lawyer has the last word for your site.

Can I bring my data from Google Analytics?

Yes. Sign in with Google and pick your property, or download a CSV from Google Analytics and add it. Plausible, GitHub releases and files from most other tools work too.

How exact is the location?

Every visitor is placed in their approximate city, from their IP address, while they're on your site; the city is never stored. If you turn it on, visitors can also choose to share their exact location, and then show on their street. It's off unless you turn it on, and they're always asked first.

Where is my data kept?

In the EU, in Frankfurt. See the privacy policy and the data processing agreement.

What happens when my trial ends?

Choose a plan to keep seeing your reports. If you don't, Statiku keeps counting for you, and everything is there when you do.

Does it work for apps?

Yes: the Swift SDK counts installs, trials and licences of your Mac and iOS apps, and matches each install to the website visit it came from.
