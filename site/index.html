Questions

Good to know.

Can I bring my data from Google Analytics?

Yes. Sign in with Google and pick your property, or download a CSV from Google Analytics and add it. Plausible, GitHub releases and files from most other tools work too.

What can I track besides my website?

Anything you can send from a script, a server or a device: installs, sales, a score, a sensor's reading, a server's status. Each value is a number, a word or a yes/no, with labels to split it by, and each tracker is charted as a line, bars, a pie or a table. See the docs.

Do heatmaps record my visitors' screens?

No. Statiku notes where on a page a click landed (and the text of the link or button) and how far down the page a visit read, and draws the heat over your real page. Nothing records the screen or what anyone types.

Can I ask questions about my numbers?

Yes. "What changed?" explains any period against the one before: the sources, pages, countries and devices behind the change, and the busiest and quietest days. Questions in your own words are answered from the same numbers.

Can I show it on a TV, or share it?

Every site has a TV view: who's on it now and where, today's conversions and takings, and a moment for every sale. Share it at a link of its own for a launch day or a team channel, and turn the link off whenever you like.

How exact is the location?

Every visitor is placed in their approximate city, from their IP address, while they're on your site; the city is never stored. If you turn it on, visitors can also choose to share their exact location, and then show on their street. It's off unless you turn it on, and they're always asked first.

Where is my data kept?

In the EU, in Stockholm, Sweden. See the privacy policy and the data processing agreement.

What happens when my trial ends?

Choose a plan to keep seeing your reports. If you don't, Statiku keeps counting for you, and everything is there when you do.

Does it work for apps?

Yes: the Swift SDK counts installs, trials and licences of your Mac and iOS apps, and matches each install to the website visit it came from.
