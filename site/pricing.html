Questions

About paying.

What counts as an event?

A page view, or anything else your sites and apps send: a download, a sign-up, an app's daily check-in. The check-ins that keep the live view current don't count.

What if I send more events than my plan has?

Statiku keeps counting, and asks you to move to the next plan. Nothing stops, and nothing is lost.

What happens when the trial ends?

Choose a plan to keep seeing your reports. If you don't, Statiku still counts your visitors, and everything is there when you do.

Can I change plans or cancel?

Yes, whenever you like, from the Plan page in Statiku. A cancelled plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

Who handles the payment?

Polar, as the merchant of record: it takes the card, adds VAT where it applies and sends the invoices. Statiku never sees your card.

Is there a discount for paying yearly?

Yes: a year costs ten months.
